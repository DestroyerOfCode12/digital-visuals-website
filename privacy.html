Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026. This page explains what information Digital Visuals collects through this website and how it's used.

Note: This is a general template, not legal advice. Digital Visuals should have this policy reviewed by a South African legal professional to confirm it fully complies with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) before relying on it.

Information We Collect

When you fill in a form on this site (such as the Contact or Waitlist forms), we collect the information you provide: your name, business name, email address, phone number, and any message or notes you include.

How We Use Your Information

We use this information only to respond to your enquiry, provide a quote, follow up about advertising space, or add you to our waitlist for upcoming locations. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Legal Basis

We process your personal information on the basis of your consent, given when you submit a form on this site, and our legitimate interest in responding to your enquiry as a business.

Data Storage & Security

Form submissions are stored securely via our website hosting provider (Netlify) and are only accessible to Digital Visuals. We take reasonable steps to protect your information from unauthorised access.

Your Rights

Under POPIA, you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information held by us. To exercise these rights, please contact us using the details on our Contact page.

Cookies & Analytics

This site may use privacy-friendly analytics to understand overall visitor traffic. We do not use tracking cookies for advertising purposes.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or your personal information, please reach out via our Contact page.
