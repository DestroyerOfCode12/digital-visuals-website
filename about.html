About Digital Visuals

Making big-city advertising visibility possible for small businesses.

We started Digital Visuals because we saw a gap: businesses were competing hard for attention, while thousands of potential customers moved past them every day on Johannesburg and Soweto's busiest roads.

MH
Our Story

Why we started Digital Visuals

Founder Mila Hobo noticed that businesses are constantly competing for people's attention, but traditional advertising is often expensive, temporary, and difficult to change once it's up. At the same time, she saw just how much foot and vehicle traffic moves through Johannesburg and Soweto every single day — and realised that those commuters represent an enormous, largely untapped advertising audience for the businesses that get overlooked by bigger agencies.

Digital Visuals was built to close that gap: high-traffic, digital billboard advertising, priced and packaged for businesses that are just getting started.
